About

Research profile in higher education, equity, and student experience.

I am a PhD Candidate in Education at the University of Ottawa. My research focuses on higher education studies, especially family structure and student experiences, educational leadership, social equity, gender, and international comparative higher education.

My current doctoral work examines the higher education experiences of students from alternative family structures. I draw on qualitative inquiry, capital theory, and social class perspectives to study how educational pathways are shaped by family background, institutional context, and social mobility.

Affiliation University of Ottawa
Program PhD in Education, 2023-present
Methods Qualitative and case study research
